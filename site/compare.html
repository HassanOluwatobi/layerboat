The tools

Overlord · $75 one time

The best-known option, from Battle Axe. It covers Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop and After Effects, and can bring Figma components in as Essential Graphics. Best if you rely on Photoshop adjustment layers and live layer styles.

UXLink · $34.99

A newer tool on aescripts + aeplugins that moves designs and animation between Figma and After Effects in both directions. Best if you animate in Figma and want those keyframes in After Effects.

AEUX · Free

The original free option, built at Google. It's no longer maintained, and it only goes from Figma into After Effects. It also supports Sketch.

Convertify · Subscription

A Figma plugin that converts designs to many formats. Its After Effects route goes through an Adobe XD file. Best if you need lots of formats beyond After Effects.

LayerBoat · Free

Free and open source. It moves layers between Figma, Illustrator and After Effects in every direction, and keeps your keyframes when you send an updated design again. Best if you want all three apps connected without paying.

How to pick

  • Need Photoshop with adjustment layers and live layer styles? Overlord. For text, shapes and images from Photoshop, LayerBoat does it free.
  • Animating in Figma first? UXLink.
  • Converting to lots of formats? Convertify.
  • Figma, Illustrator and After Effects, for free? LayerBoat.

Questions

What is the best free Figma to After Effects plugin?

LayerBoat is free, open source and maintained, and works in every direction between Figma, Illustrator and After Effects. AEUX is also free but no longer maintained.

Can I get Figma animation into After Effects?

UXLink carries keyframes and easing between Figma and After Effects. The other tools here move the design so you animate it in After Effects.

Which tool supports Photoshop?

Overlord and LayerBoat both send Photoshop layers to After Effects. Overlord also carries adjustment layers and live layer styles. LayerBoat is free.
