Overlord by Battle Axe is the best-known way to bring designs into After Effects. LayerBoat does the same core job between Figma, Illustrator and After Effects, for free. Here's how the two compare.

LayerBoat is a good fit if

  • You work between Figma, Illustrator and After Effects.
  • You want it free, on as many machines as you like.
  • You'd rather not deal with licence keys or renewals.
  • You like being able to read or change the code.

Overlord is a good fit if

  • You need Photoshop layers in After Effects.
  • You want Figma components as Essential Graphics.
  • You want paid support and a Discord community.
