Move your designs between Figma, Illustrator and After Effects.

Send a Figma frame to After Effects and it lands as shape layers and text you can animate. Send it back when you're done. Free, with the code in the open.

Download LayerBoat

LayerBoat runs on a Mac or PC. Open this page there to install it.

For Mac and Windows · After Effects, Illustrator and Figma

Features

What comes across

LayerBoat rebuilds your design with each app's own tools. You get layers you can work with, not a picture of them.

Shapes stay shapes

Rectangles, ellipses, stars, polygons and pen paths become native shape layers, with their fills, strokes, dashes and rounded corners.

Text stays editable

Every text box arrives as a real text layer with its font, size, spacing and line height. Change the words in After Effects and keep going.

Mixed styles in one box need After Effects 2024.3 or newer

Gradients, images and masks

Linear and radial gradients, image fills and clipping masks all come with your layers.

Your names and groups

Layer names carry over. Groups become parent layers you can move together, and one click turns a group into its own precomp.

Send again, keep your animation

Changed the design? Send the frame again. LayerBoat rebuilds it in the same comp and leaves your keyframes, effects and added layers alone.

Round trips

Send a comp back to Figma, or open it in Illustrator on a new artboard with names and editable text intact.

Swap one shape

Redrew a shape in Illustrator? Update from Illustrator replaces that one path in your comp, so you don't have to rebuild the layer.

An image when you need one

For effects that only Illustrator can draw, As an image brings the artwork in as a single sharp image layer in its place.

Nothing leaves your computer

Designs travel through a small helper on your own machine. There's no account and nothing is uploaded.

How to use

Every direction, step by step

Keep the LayerBoat panel open in After Effects (Window › LayerBoat.jsx) and the LayerBoat panel open in Illustrator. In Figma, run the plugin from Plugins › Development › LayerBoat.

Figma → After Effects

  1. In After Effects, tick Receive live from Figma in the LayerBoat panel.
  2. In Figma, select a frame and open the LayerBoat plugin.
  3. Click Send to After Effects. The frame opens as a new comp.

Want it in the comp you're working in? Tick Paste into the open comp first.

After Effects → Figma

  1. In Figma, open the LayerBoat plugin and leave it open.
  2. In After Effects, open the comp. Select layers to send only those.
  3. Click Send comp to Figma. It appears in Figma as a frame.

Illustrator → After Effects

  1. In Illustrator, select artwork. Select nothing to send the whole artboard.
  2. In After Effects, click Bring in from Illustrator in the LayerBoat panel.
  3. The artwork opens as a new comp, with text still editable.

Or work from Illustrator: tick Receive live in After Effects, then click Send to After Effects in Illustrator's LayerBoat panel.

After Effects → Illustrator

  1. Open Illustrator.
  2. In After Effects, open the comp. Select layers to send only those.
  3. Click Send comp to Illustrator. It lands on a new artboard.

Figma → Illustrator

  1. In Figma, select a frame and click Send to Illustrator in the plugin.
  2. In Illustrator, click Bring in from Figma in the LayerBoat panel.
  3. The frame lands on a new artboard, or in a new document if none is open.

Illustrator → Figma

  1. In Figma, open the LayerBoat plugin and leave it open.
  2. In Illustrator, select artwork, or nothing for the whole artboard.
  3. Click Send to Figma in Illustrator's LayerBoat panel.

Install

Set up once, in four steps

The installer in step 2 sets up all three apps. Steps 3 and 4 connect Figma and Illustrator to it.

  1. Download and unzip.You get a folder called LayerBoat. Keep it until you've finished step 4.
  2. Run the installer in After Effects.Go to File › Scripts › Run Script File and pick Install LayerBoat.jsx. Use Run Script File. The option called Install ScriptUI Panel is a different one and won't work. The panel opens straight away, and lives under Window › LayerBoat.jsx from then on.
  3. Add the Figma plugin.In Figma, go to Plugins › Development › Import plugin from manifest. The installer shows the file to pick and has a Copy path button.
  4. Open the Illustrator panel.In Illustrator, go to File › Scripts › Other Script and pick LayerBoat Panel.jsx in the folder the installer shows. Keep the panel open beside your artwork.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier. The code is MIT licensed, so anyone can read it, change it or build on it.

Does it upload my designs anywhere?

No. Your designs move between apps through a helper running on your own computer. Nothing is sent online.

Which versions does it work with?

It's tested with After Effects and Illustrator 2026 and 2027 on a Mac, and the current Figma desktop app. Older versions back to 2022 should work. Text with mixed styles in one box needs After Effects 2024.3 or newer.

Does it work on Windows?

It's built for Windows and passes our Windows tests, but nobody has tried it on a real PC yet. If you do, please tell us how it went on GitHub.

Nothing arrives in After Effects. What do I check?

Make sure Receive live from Figma is ticked in the panel, and that Settings › Scripting & Expressions › Allow Scripts to Write Files and Access Network is on. You can also click Copy instead in the Figma plugin, then Paste from Figma in After Effects.

How is this different from Overlord?

Overlord is a paid, polished tool that also covers Photoshop. LayerBoat is free and open source, and covers Figma, Illustrator and After Effects.

How do I remove it?

Run Uninstall LayerBoat.jsx from the download the same way you ran the installer. Then remove the plugin in Figma under Plugins › Development › Manage plugins in development.

I found a bug.

Open an issue on GitHub. Say which apps you used, what you sent and what came out. A screenshot helps a lot.
