LayerBoat vs Convertify

Convertify is a popular Figma plugin that converts designs into many formats, After Effects among them. LayerBoat does one job, moving layers between Figma, Illustrator and After Effects. Here's how they compare for motion work.

A quick note before we start. I make LayerBoat, so read this with that in mind. I've kept every fact about the other tool to what its makers publish, and I've linked my sources at the end.

The short answer. If you need to convert Figma files to lots of formats, Convertify does far more than LayerBoat. If you only need Figma and Illustrator in After Effects, LayerBoat goes straight there, in both directions, for free.

How each one gets your design into After Effects

Convertify, by Hypermatic, converts a Figma page into an Adobe XD file. You then open that file in XD and use its own export to send the artboard to After Effects. It's a two-step route through a third app.

LayerBoat sends layers straight from Figma into After Effects. There's no file in between and no other app to open.

Price

Convertify includes 10 free Pro trials. After that, the After Effects workflow needs Convertify Pro, which costs $49 a month or $499 a year per user.

LayerBoat is free.

Where Convertify is ahead

  • Many formats. Convertify exports Figma to Sketch, XD, After Effects and more, and imports XD, Illustrator, PDF and Google Docs into Figma.
  • In the Figma Community. It installs with one click from Figma.

Where LayerBoat is ahead

  • Straight into After Effects. No Adobe XD step in the middle.
  • Both directions. Send a comp from After Effects back to Figma or Illustrator.
  • Built for motion. Sending an updated frame again keeps your keyframes and effects.
  • Cost. Free, with no subscription.

Which should you choose?

Pick Convertify if converting between many design formats is part of your job and the subscription pays for itself. Pick LayerBoat if your goal is animating Figma or Illustrator work in After Effects.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to Convertify for After Effects?

Yes. LayerBoat sends Figma and Illustrator designs straight into After Effects for free.

Does Convertify need Adobe XD?

Its After Effects workflow converts your design to an Adobe XD file first, then uses XD's export to After Effects.

Can LayerBoat convert to Sketch or PDF?

No. LayerBoat only moves layers between Figma, Illustrator and After Effects.

LayerBoat is free for Mac and Windows. It takes about five minutes to set up.
